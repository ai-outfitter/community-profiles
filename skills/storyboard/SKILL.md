# Storyboard

Storyboard before you build the artifact. The storyboard is the narrative
source; the deck or post is a rendering of it.

## Process

1. State the story's thesis in one sentence.
2. List the beats in order. One claim per beat. Five to nine beats.
3. Pair each beat with one prose line (the claim as the audience hears it)
   and one image prompt (what the audience sees).
4. Write `storyboard.md` as a table: `beat | claim | prose | image prompt`.
5. Hand the file to the artifact skill: `slidev` for a deck, or draft the
   one-pager or pre-release from the prose column.

## Hard limits

- Do not draft the artifact before the storyboard exists.
- Do not put two claims in one beat; split the beat.
